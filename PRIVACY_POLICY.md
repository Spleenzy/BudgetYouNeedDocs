# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 July 2026

[Polska wersja / Polish version](PRIVACY_POLICY_PL.md)

### 1. Controller

The controller for data related to the **Budget You Need (BYN)** application
is **Patryk Woźniak operating under the name Itly**, Poland, Blasku Księżyca
20/2, 05-500 Łoziska.

Contact: [itly.company.pl@gmail.com](mailto:itly.company.pl@gmail.com).

### 2. Scope

This policy explains how data is handled when using the BYN mobile application
on Android and iOS.

### 3. Data stored locally

BYN is a local-first application. Financial data, including income, payments,
expenses, budgets, categories, loans and settings, is stored only on the
user's device in an encrypted database.

The encryption key is stored in Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. BYN does not
send this data to its own servers.

### 4. Receipt photos and attachments

Users may optionally attach a receipt photo, image, or PDF to an expense. These
attachments are stored locally on the device in encrypted form. BYN does not
upload attachments to its own servers. Camera access is requested only when a
user chooses to take a receipt photo.

### 5. Biometrics and app lock

The optional biometric app lock uses the operating system's authentication
mechanisms, such as Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint authentication. BYN does
not receive or store fingerprints, face images or other raw biometric data. The
app receives only the result of the system authentication.

### 6. Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on the device. BYN does not use its
own push server and does not send notification content to a server.
Notification content may be visible according to the operating system's
settings, including on the lock screen.

### 7. Backup export and import

Users may voluntarily export an encrypted copy of their data and transfer it
outside the device, for example to a file, cloud storage or another app. BYN
does not store these backups on its own servers and has no control over the
destination selected by the user.

The user and the provider of the selected service are responsible for backups
stored outside the application.

### 8. Email contact

Users may voluntarily contact the controller through the contact form available
in the app. The form opens the user's email application and does not send a
message automatically. Once sent, the message and sender details are processed
by the user's email provider and the controller to handle the request.

### 9. Support-contact data, purpose and legal basis

When users contact us, we may process the contact data they voluntarily provide,
such as their email address, name, message content, and any attachment. We use
this data only to answer and manage the support, privacy, or security request,
maintain correspondence where necessary, and establish, exercise, or defend
legal claims.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the legal basis
is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR when the request relates to a contract or steps before
entering into a contract, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interest
in communicating with users, maintaining security, and handling claims. If a
specific processing activity relies on consent, its basis is Article 6(1)(a)
GDPR. BYN does not make automated decisions or profile users using this data.

### 10. Recipients and international transfers

Support correspondence is processed through email service providers, including
Google Gmail. These providers act under their own terms and privacy notices and
may process email data outside the European Economic Area. Where such a transfer
occurs, it is subject to the transfer safeguards made available by the provider
under applicable data-protection law. We do not sell personal data or disclose
it for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may disclose contact data to competent public authorities only where this is
required by applicable law. BYN financial records and attachments are not sent
to these providers by the app.

### 11. Retention of support correspondence

We retain support, privacy, and security correspondence only for as long as
needed to handle the request and maintain an appropriate record of the matter.
We then delete or anonymize it, unless a longer retention period is necessary
to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

### 12. Analytics, advertising and crash reporting

BYN does not use analytics, advertising or third-party crash reporting.
Financial data is not used for profiling or marketing.

### 13. Data deletion

Because app data is stored locally, users can delete it through the operating
system's app settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data according to
the applicable operating system's rules.

Backups exported outside the device must be deleted separately from every place
where the user stored them.

### 14. User rights and contact

For privacy-related matters, contact the controller at
[itly.company.pl@gmail.com](mailto:itly.company.pl@gmail.com). The request
should describe the matter and provide enough information to receive a reply.
If a user believes their rights have been infringed, they may also contact the
competent data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, users may request access to, rectification or erasure
of contact data, restriction of processing, data portability where applicable,
or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send such a request to
[itly.company.pl@gmail.com](mailto:itly.company.pl@gmail.com). Users may also
complain to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in
Poland or another competent data-protection authority.

### 15. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app's operation or applicable requirements
change. The current version will be published on the public BYN documentation
site and in the app. For material changes, the effective date will be updated.
