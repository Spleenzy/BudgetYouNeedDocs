1. Controller

The controller for data related to the Budget You Need (BYN) application is Patryk Woźniak operating under the name Itly, Poland, Blasku Księżyca 20/2, 05-500 Łoziska. Contact: itly.company.pl@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy explains how data is handled when using the BYN mobile application on Android and iOS.

3. Data stored locally

BYN is a local-first application. Financial data, including income, payments, expenses, budgets, categories, loans and settings, is stored only on the user's device in an encrypted database. The encryption key is stored in Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. BYN does not send this data to its own servers.

4. Receipt photos and attachments

Users may optionally attach a receipt photo, image, or PDF to an expense. Attachments are stored locally on the device in encrypted form and are not uploaded to BYN servers. Camera access is requested only when a user chooses to take a receipt photo.

5. Biometrics and app lock

The optional biometric app lock uses the operating system's authentication mechanisms, such as Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint authentication. BYN does not receive or store fingerprints, face images or other raw biometric data. The app receives only the result of the system authentication.

6. Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on the device. BYN does not use its own push server and does not send notification content to a server. Notification content may be visible according to the operating system's settings, including on the lock screen.

7. Backup export and import

Users may voluntarily export an encrypted copy of their data and transfer it outside the device, for example to a file, cloud storage or another app. BYN does not store these backups on its own servers and has no control over the destination selected by the user.

The user and the provider of the selected service are responsible for backups stored outside the application.

8. Email contact

Users may voluntarily contact the controller through the contact form available in the app. The form opens the user's email application and does not send a message automatically. Once sent, the message and sender details are processed by the user's email provider and the controller to handle the request.

9. Support-contact data, purpose and legal basis

When users contact us, we may process the contact data they voluntarily provide, such as their email address, name, message content, and any attachment. We use this data only to handle the request, maintain correspondence where necessary, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) for contract-related requests and Article 6(1)(f) for legitimate interests in communication, security, and claims. Where processing relies on consent, the basis is Article 6(1)(a). BYN does not make automated decisions or profile users using this data.

10. Recipients and international transfers

Support correspondence is processed through email service providers, including Google Gmail. They may process email data outside the European Economic Area under safeguards available under applicable data-protection law. We do not sell personal data or disclose it for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may disclose contact data to competent public authorities only where required by law. The app does not send financial records or attachments to these providers.

11. Retention of support correspondence

We retain support, privacy, and security correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request and maintain an appropriate record of the matter. We then delete or anonymize it, unless longer retention is necessary for a legal obligation or legal claims.

12. Analytics, advertising and crash reporting

BYN does not use analytics, advertising or third-party crash reporting. Financial data is not used for profiling or marketing.

13. Data deletion

Because app data is stored locally, users can delete it through the operating system's app settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data according to the applicable operating system's rules. Backups exported outside the device must be deleted separately from every place where the user stored them.

14. User rights and contact

For privacy-related matters, contact the controller at itly.company.pl@gmail.com. Where the GDPR applies, users may request access to, rectification or erasure of contact data, restriction, portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Users may also complain to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland or another competent authority.

15. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app's operation or applicable requirements change. The current version is published on this website and in the public GitHub document. For material changes, the effective date will be updated.

Note: This document describes the app's current technical data practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice or a review against the controller's exact legal status and obligations.
